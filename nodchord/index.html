Calibrate

Your hands hold a shape and a nod plays it. Calibration first learns how big your nod is, then how far apart your fingers sit in two piano shapes. Both hands are captured together, but one hand alone also works.

  1. Nod three timesA normal, clear nod. This sets how big a nod has to be.
  2. Hands in the outlinesUntil the distance gauge turns green, then hold for a second.
  3. Five-finger shape · nod ×4Right: thumb C to pinky G. Left: pinky C to thumb G. Relax and re-form the shape between nods.
  4. C–E–G–C shape · nod ×4Right: thumb C, index E, middle G, pinky high C. Left: pinky C, middle E, index G, thumb high C.

Play

Shape a chord with one or both hands, then nod. Each thumb is a root; the other fingers play the key as far from the thumb as they are spread, upward for the right hand and downward for the left.

Curl a finger into the palm to leave it out of the chord. The notes above your fingertips show what the next nod will play.

Right root ←→ white keys, ↑↓ octave. Left root AD white keys, WS octave. Hold Shift to move by semitones. Click a key for the right root, right-click for the left.
